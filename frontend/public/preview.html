Participation Model

Two distinct, dedicated paths

Mentorship is verified per course after technical review. Learner access and mentor privileges remain cleanly separated.

Join as a Learner

Work through structured lessons, complete practical checkpoints, and ask mentors for guidance when stuck.

  • 1 Create your verified learner profile
  • 2 Select a course roadmap matching your goal
  • 3 Build working deliverables each chapter

Apply to Mentor a Course

Practicing engineers and educators apply to lead specific courses. Approved mentors receive a dedicated authoring workspace.

  • Submit your practical background & resume
  • Short technical verification interview
  • Publish sessions, chapter notes & guides
